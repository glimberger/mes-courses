# Feature Specification: Multi-User Accounts

**Feature Branch**: `feat/004-multi-user`

**Created**: 2026-10-12

**Status**: Implemented

**Input**: User description: "gestion multi-utilisateurs. The application is open to other users; they sign in with their email. On registration they receive an email containing their credentials."

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-12

- Q: Is the data private to each user, or shared? → A: Private. Each account has its own lists,
  articles and categories, invisible to every other account. Sharing lists between accounts stays
  out of scope.
- Q: Who can register? → A: Only people the maintainer invites. There is no public sign-up: the
  maintainer enters an email address and the invited person receives their credentials by email.
- Q: What does the registration email contain, and how is the password handled? → A: The email
  address (the sign-in name) and a temporary password generated by the server. The temporary
  password must be replaced by a password of the user's choice at the first sign-in. A user who
  forgot their password can ask for a new temporary password by email.
- Q: How does signing in relate to the device pairing code of 003? → A: Signing in with email and
  password replaces the pairing code: any device is authorized by signing in. The list of devices,
  their renaming and their revocation (003) remain, per account. The devices already paired on the
  server become those of the maintainer's account.
- Q: How are accounts managed? → A: In the app. The maintainer's account has an administrator
  role with a screen to invite people by email, list accounts, and suspend or delete one.
- Q: What happens to the data that exists before this feature? → A: It is attached to the
  maintainer's account, which is created from it, with no loss.
- Q: What does signing in change while offline? → A: Nothing for a device already signed in: it
  stays signed in and fully usable without a network (constitution Principle VII). Suspension,
  deletion or revocation is applied at the next contact with the server.
- Q: Can a user delete their own account? → A: Yes, from the settings, after a confirmation. The
  account and all its data on the server are deleted.
- Q: When a new temporary password is requested from the sign-in screen, does the current password
  stop working at once? → A: No. The current password keeps working until the new temporary
  password is used for the first time; the temporary password then replaces it. An unused
  temporary password expires after 7 days and the current password stays valid.

**Depends on**: [003-server-sync](../003-server-sync/spec.md), which this feature extends from one
user with several devices to several users with their own devices. It replaces the pairing code
(003 FR-019a) with an account sign-in and keeps the other device rules of 003 (authorized devices
list, rename, revoke, disconnect).

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - The maintainer invites someone (Priority: P1)

As the administrator, I enter the email address of a person I want to let use the app. They
receive an email with their sign-in name and a temporary password, so that they can start without
any other step from me.

**Why this priority**: without invitations there is no second user; everything else builds on it.

**Independent Test**: signed in as administrator, invite an address, check the email sent (with a
test double of the mail sending) contains the address and a temporary password, then sign in with
them.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am signed in as administrator, **When** I enter a valid email address that has no account and confirm, **Then** an account is created for it, an email with the address and a temporary password is sent, and the account appears in the account list as "invited, not yet signed in".
2. **Given** I enter an address that already has an account, **When** I confirm, **Then** no second account is created, no email is sent, and a French message says that this address already has an account.
3. **Given** I enter an address that is not a valid email address, **When** I confirm, **Then** a French message says so and nothing is created or sent.
4. **Given** the email cannot be sent (mail service down), **When** I confirm, **Then** the account is not left half created: a French message says that the invitation failed, and I can try again.
5. **Given** I am signed in as an ordinary user, **When** I look for the account management screen, **Then** it is not offered, and the server refuses any attempt to invite, list, suspend or delete accounts.

---

### User Story 2 - First sign-in with the temporary password (Priority: P1)

As an invited person, I install the app, sign in with the address and the temporary password from
the email, choose my own password, and land in my empty space ready to use.

**Why this priority**: it is the other half of the invitation; together they make the feature
usable by someone other than the maintainer.

**Independent Test**: with an invited account, sign in with the temporary password, set a new
password, check the temporary one no longer works and the new one does.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I received my credentials, **When** I enter my email and the temporary password on a device, **Then** I am asked to choose a new password before anything else.
2. **Given** I am asked to choose a password, **When** I enter one that meets the rules (at least 12 characters, different from the temporary one) twice, identical, **Then** it is saved, the temporary password stops working, and I enter the app with empty lists of my own.
3. **Given** the two entries differ or the password is too short, **When** I confirm, **Then** a French message says what is wrong, and nothing changes.
4. **Given** the email or the password is wrong, **When** I try to sign in, **Then** a French message says the credentials are not valid, without telling which of the two is wrong.
5. **Given** the temporary password has not been used within 7 days, **When** I try to sign in with it, **Then** it is refused with a French message saying it has expired and that I can ask for a new one (User Story 4).
6. **Given** I have signed in once, **When** I reopen the app later, even without a network, **Then** I am still signed in and go straight to my lists.

---

### User Story 3 - My data stays mine (Priority: P1)

As a user, I only ever see and change my own lists, articles and categories, on all my devices, and
nobody else sees mine.

**Why this priority**: opening the app to other people is only acceptable if their data is
separated; this is the security core of the feature.

**Independent Test**: two accounts each create lists and articles; each one, on a fresh device,
syncs and gets only its own data; requests for the other account's data are refused.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** two users with data, **When** each syncs, **Then** each receives only their own data and the other's is never sent to their device.
2. **Given** I sign in on a second device, **When** the first sync ends, **Then** it holds exactly my data, as in 003.
3. **Given** a device signed in as user A, **When** it tries to read or change data belonging to user B, **Then** the server refuses and nothing is read or changed.
4. **Given** two users who both have an article called "Lait", **When** either changes or deletes theirs, **Then** the other's is not affected.
5. **Given** the data that existed before this feature, **When** the feature is deployed, **Then** it all belongs to the maintainer's account and the maintainer's devices keep working without signing in again.

---

### User Story 4 - I forgot my password (Priority: P2)

As a user who cannot sign in, I ask for a new temporary password to be sent to my email address
and get back in.

**Why this priority**: invited people are not technical and will forget passwords; without it the
maintainer must intervene each time.

**Independent Test**: ask for a reset for a known address with a test double of the mail sending,
check the email, sign in with the new temporary password and choose a new password.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am on the sign-in screen, **When** I enter my address and ask for a new password, **Then** a French message says that if an account exists for this address, an email was sent, whether or not the account exists.
2. **Given** an account exists for the address, **When** I ask, **Then** an email with a new temporary password is sent, my current password keeps working until I sign in with the temporary one, and when I do I must choose a new password, as in User Story 2, which replaces both.
3. **Given** no account exists for the address, **When** I ask, **Then** no email is sent and the screen shows the same message as above.
4. **Given** I asked many times in a row, **When** I ask again within a short time, **Then** the request is refused with a French message asking to wait, so that the feature cannot be used to flood someone's mailbox.
5. **Given** someone else asked for a new temporary password for my address, **When** I sign in with my current password, **Then** it works as before and the unused temporary password expires after 7 days.
6. **Given** my account is suspended, **When** I ask for a new password, **Then** no email is sent and the screen shows the same message as in scenario 1.

---

### User Story 5 - The administrator manages accounts (Priority: P2)

As the administrator, I see the list of accounts and I can suspend one, give it back, or delete it,
for example when someone should no longer use my server.

**Why this priority**: the server is the maintainer's Raspberry Pi; they must be able to stop
using it by someone without touching the server by hand.

**Independent Test**: with an administrator and an ordinary user, suspend the user and check their
device is refused at the next sync; lift the suspension and check it works again.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am signed in as administrator, **When** I open the account screen, **Then** I see every account with its email, whether it is active, invited, or suspended, and when it last synced.
2. **Given** an active account, **When** I suspend it and confirm, **Then** its devices are refused at their next contact with the server, keep their local copy, and show a French message saying the account is suspended; its data on the server is kept.
3. **Given** a suspended account, **When** I lift the suspension, **Then** its devices sync again with no sign-in needed and no data lost.
4. **Given** an account, **When** I delete it and confirm, **Then** the account and all its data on the server are deleted, and its devices, at their next contact, are signed out with a French message and keep nothing of the account's data on screen after signing out (User Story 6, scenario 5).
5. **Given** I am the only administrator, **When** I try to suspend or delete my own account, **Then** it is refused with a French message, so that the server is never left with no administrator.
6. **Given** an account that was invited but never signed in, **When** I use "send the credentials again", **Then** a new temporary password is emailed and the previous one stops working. If the email cannot be sent, the previous temporary password keeps working and a French message says the sending failed.

---

### User Story 6 - My account and my devices (Priority: P2)

As a user, I see which account I am signed in with, change my password, sign out of this device,
manage my devices, and delete my account if I no longer want to use the app.

**Why this priority**: the account must be controllable by its owner; deletion also answers the
legitimate expectation of people other than the maintainer to be able to leave.

**Independent Test**: sign in, change the password, list devices, revoke one, delete the account
and check the data is gone from the server.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** I am signed in, **When** I open the settings, **Then** I see my email address, the server, when I last synced, and my authorized devices with their names and last sync (003 FR-016, FR-019c), only mine.
2. **Given** I am signed in, **When** I change my password by entering the current one and a new one that meets the rules, **Then** the password is changed, and my other devices stay signed in.
3. **Given** I am signed in, **When** I revoke another of my devices, **Then** it is refused at once at its next contact, as in 003, and keeps its local copy until the user has seen the message that tells it was revoked (FR-017).
4. **Given** I am signed in, **When** I sign out of this device and confirm, **Then** the device's credential and the local copy of my data are removed from it, the device is revoked on the server if it is reachable, and the sign-in screen is shown. If changes are still waiting to be sent, I am warned that they will be lost and must confirm.
5. **Given** a device signed out by deletion (including the deletion of a suspended account) or revocation, **When** it learns of it, **Then** a French message says why, and the data of the account is no longer shown after the user has seen the message.
6. **Given** I am signed in, **When** I delete my account and confirm with my password, **Then** the account and all its data on the server are deleted, all its devices are signed out at their next contact, and I am shown the sign-in screen.
7. **Given** I am the only administrator, **When** I try to delete my account, **Then** it is refused as in User Story 5, scenario 5.

---

### Edge Cases

- A device is signed in while offline for weeks: it keeps working on its local copy (003 FR-003). If its account was suspended or deleted meanwhile, it finds out at its next contact with the server.
- The same person signs in on a second device with the temporary password after already choosing a new one: the temporary password no longer works; a French message says the credentials are not valid.
- Two devices of one user change data offline, then sync: reconciled by the rules of 003, which apply per account.
- An email address is typed with different capitals or spaces around it: it is treated as the same address (case-insensitive, trimmed).
- Many failed sign-in attempts in a row on one account or from one device: further attempts are slowed down or refused for a while with a French message, so that passwords cannot be guessed by trial.
- The administrator invites an address that belongs to a person who deleted their account earlier: a new, empty account is created; nothing of the old one comes back.
- A user with pending, unsent changes signs out or has their account deleted: the user is warned before signing out voluntarily; on deletion, those changes are discarded because their account no longer exists.
- The server resets (003, lost data): accounts are lost with the data; the maintainer's account is recreated at setup and invited users are invited again. This feature provides no backup (003 FR-018a).
- A user signs in on a device that still holds another account's local copy (the previous user did not sign out): the local copy of the previous account is removed before the new one syncs; the two accounts' data are never mixed on a device.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The server MUST hold several accounts. Each account is identified by one email address, unique across the server, compared without regard to case or surrounding spaces.
- **FR-002**: There MUST be no public registration. An account is created only when the administrator invites an email address, or for the maintainer's own account at setup (FR-015).
- **FR-003**: When an account is invited, the server MUST send one email to the address, written in French, containing the sign-in name (the address) and a temporary password generated by the server. If the email cannot be sent, the account MUST NOT be created (or MUST be removed) and the administrator MUST be told that the invitation failed.
- **FR-004**: A temporary password MUST be random, valid until its first successful use or 7 days, whichever comes first, and usable only to reach the "choose a password" step (FR-005). Passwords MUST never be stored or logged in a readable form, and MUST never appear in error reports (constitution Principle VIII).
- **FR-005**: At the first sign-in with a temporary password, the user MUST be asked to choose a password before using the app. A chosen password MUST have at least 12 characters and differ from the temporary one; it MUST be entered twice, identically.
- **FR-006**: Users MUST sign in with their email and password. A refused sign-in MUST NOT reveal whether the address has an account. Repeated failed attempts MUST be slowed down or refused for a while, for an account and for a device (the client's network address).
- **FR-007**: A device that is signed in MUST stay signed in until the user signs out or the server ends its access (FR-010, FR-012, FR-014), and MUST remain fully usable offline, with no sign-in required (constitution Principle VII). Signing in itself requires reaching the server.
- **FR-008**: Signing in MUST authorize the device on the server under that account, replaces the pairing code of 003 (003 FR-019a), and makes the device appear in the account's device list with a name (the device model by default, which the user can change).
- **FR-009**: Every piece of user data (lists, items, articles, categories and their order) MUST belong to exactly one account. The server MUST send a device only the data of its own account and MUST refuse any request that reads or changes the data of another account. Reconciliation rules of 003 apply within one account.
- **FR-010**: Users MUST be able to ask for a new temporary password by email from the sign-in screen. The answer shown MUST be the same whether or not the address has an account, no email MUST be sent to an address with no account or to a suspended account, the current password MUST keep working until the new temporary password is first used (which then replaces it), so that a request made by someone else cannot lock the owner out, and requests MUST be limited in frequency per address.
- **FR-011**: Users MUST be able to change their password (current password required), see their email address, and sign out of the device. Signing out MUST destroy the device's credential, remove the account's local copy from the device, and warn the user first if changes are still waiting to be sent. When the server is reachable, signing out also revokes the device on the server. Offline, the device stays in the account's device list, unusable because its credential is gone, until the owner revokes it from another device.
- **FR-012**: Users MUST be able to see, rename and revoke their own devices only (003 FR-019c). A revoked device MUST be refused at its next contact.
- **FR-013**: The administrator role MUST belong to at least one account at all times and MUST give access to: inviting an email address, listing accounts (email, state active / invited / suspended, last sync), suspending and reactivating an account, deleting an account, and sending the credentials again to an invited account (a new temporary password, the previous one stops working). Only administrators MUST be able to do these things; the server MUST refuse them from any other account.
- **FR-014**: A suspended account's devices MUST be refused at their next contact, keep their local copy, and show a French message; its data MUST be kept on the server. Reactivating the account MUST let its devices sync again with no new sign-in.
- **FR-015**: At the deployment of this feature, the server MUST create the maintainer's account, with the administrator role, and attach to it all the existing data and all the devices already authorized. The maintainer's devices MUST keep syncing with no new sign-in. The maintainer's address and initial password are provided at setup, not through an invitation email.
- **FR-016**: Users MUST be able to delete their own account from the settings, after a confirmation with their password. Deleting an account MUST delete all its data and devices on the server, and its devices MUST be signed out at their next contact. An administrator MUST be able to delete any other account. An account that is the only administrator MUST NOT be deletable or suspendable (FR-013).
- **FR-017**: A device signed out by the server (deletion, revocation) MUST tell the user why in French, and MUST NOT keep showing the account's data once the message was seen. A device MUST NEVER hold the data of two accounts at once: signing in as a different account removes the previous account's local copy first.
- **FR-018**: The sync status, the offline behaviour and the error reporting of 003 (FR-020, FR-022a) MUST keep working. Error reports MUST NOT contain email addresses, passwords, or any other personal data; account identifiers in reports MUST be pseudonymous (constitution Principle VIII).
- **FR-019**: All user-facing text, including the emails, MUST be in French (constitution Principle X). Every new screen MUST handle loading, empty and failure states and use only the shared design system components (constitution Principles V and IX).
- **FR-020**: Sending the emails MUST go through a driven port (constitution Principle VI), so that tests use a test double and no test sends a real email. A failure of the mail service MUST be reported to the error tracking tool and MUST NOT break the app or the server.

### Key Entities

- **Account**: a person allowed to use the server. Has an email address (unique), a role (user or administrator), a state (invited, active, suspended), a password (or a temporary password to be replaced), and the date of its last sync.
- **Temporary password**: a generated, single-purpose password sent by email, replaced at first sign-in, expiring after 7 days. An account has at most one at a time; for an invited account it is the only password, for an active account it coexists with the current password until used or expired.
- **Authorized device**: as in 003, now belonging to exactly one account: a name, the date of its last sync, and whether it is revoked.
- **User data**: the lists, items, articles and categories of 001, 002 and 003, now each owned by one account.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: An invited person goes from receiving the email to seeing their own empty lists in under 3 minutes, with no help from the maintainer.
- **SC-002**: The administrator invites a new person in under 30 seconds from the account screen.
- **SC-003**: 100% of requests that read or change the data of another account are refused, and no data of one account is ever sent to a device of another.
- **SC-004**: 100% of requests from suspended, deleted or revoked accounts and devices are refused at their next contact, and a device that is already signed in keeps working offline for as long as the server is not reached.
- **SC-005**: After the deployment of this feature, 100% of the maintainer's existing data and devices are still available and keep syncing with no sign-in.
- **SC-006**: A user who forgot their password is back in the app in under 3 minutes without the maintainer's help.
- **SC-007**: After an account is deleted, none of its data remains on the server; the named remnants of [research.md](research.md) R16 are not its data.
- **SC-008**: No password, temporary password or email address appears in any error report or server log used for diagnosis.

## Assumptions

- The server is still one Raspberry Pi run by the maintainer (003); it now needs a way to send email. Which mail service is used, and how it is set up, is a plan decision. The maintainer accepts that the Pi's availability is the only limit to how many accounts it can serve; a small number of users (about 10) is assumed.
- Invited people are known to the maintainer; there is no email verification beyond the fact that the credentials are sent to the address and a temporary password must be replaced.
- Exactly one administrator exists at first (the maintainer). Making another account an administrator is out of scope; the rule that one administrator always remains still applies (FR-013, FR-016).
- Sharing lists, articles or categories between accounts, teams or households, and importing another account's data, are out of scope and may be a later feature.
- Sign-in with a social account, single sign-on, two-factor authentication and biometric unlock are out of scope.
- Changing the email address of an account is out of scope: the address is the identity of the account. A person who needs another address is invited again and the old account is deleted.
- Password rules (12 characters minimum) and limits on attempts and requests (FR-006, FR-010) have sensible values that the plan sets precisely and that tests verify.
- The French wording of every message is defined at the planning stage, like in 003.
